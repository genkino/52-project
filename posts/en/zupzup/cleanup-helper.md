Saving everything you come across in ZupZup isn’t always the answer. There’s an article to read later, a link worth coming back to, a screenshot you took so you wouldn’t forget something. Save them one at a time and, before you know it, there’s quite a pile. Letting go of a few things you no longer need makes it easier to find the ones you do.

But going through your entire collection is a chore. That’s why we’re building Cleanup Helper for ZupZup 1.17: a place to start with duplicates, items you haven’t opened in a while, and items that take up a lot of space.

> **These screenshots are from an in-development build of ZupZup 1.17.** We’re still trying it out and refining it. The screens, button labels, and steps may change before release.

![The Tools tab lists Cleanup Helper separately, below Screenshot Cleanup.](/media/zupzup-guides/cleanup-helper/01-tools.png)

*Open Cleanup Helper from the Tools tab. It’s separate from Screenshot Cleanup.*

## When you save the same thing twice

Sometimes you forget you’ve saved something and save it again. Duplicates groups items that look like copies. The idea is to open one group at a time and decide what to keep, rather than jumping around the whole list.

![The Duplicates tab in Cleanup Helper groups items that appear to be copies.](/media/zupzup-guides/cleanup-helper/02-duplicates-redacted.png)

*In Duplicates, review the groups instead of hunting for individual copies. One sample item title has been hidden.*

## When “read later” turns into much later

In Unopened, you can look back 30 days, 90 days, 180 days, or a year. Not opening something for months doesn’t mean you don’t need it. We’re making it possible to use the tags you’ve already added to keep important items out of the cleanup list.

![The Unopened tab with 180 days selected and the Excluded Tags control above the list.](/media/zupzup-guides/cleanup-helper/03-unopened.png)

*Choose a time range in Unopened. Use Excluded Tags above the list to choose which tagged items to leave out.*

## When you want to keep something that takes up space

Large shows you which items take up the most space. For items that contain photos, we’re working on a separate Reduce Image Size option, so deletion isn’t your only choice. The idea is to compare the images and file sizes before and after, then decide whether to keep the original or reduce its size.

![The Large tab lists each item’s size, with a separate Reduce Image Size button above the list.](/media/zupzup-guides/cleanup-helper/04-large.png)

*In Large, check the size shown for each item. Reduce Image Size is a separate option above the list.*

Cleanup Helper doesn’t decide what to throw away. It gives you somewhere to start; you choose what to keep and what to let go of. Finding an unread article again is as much a part of tidying up as clearing out something you no longer need.
