Keeping a photo of a paper document makes it easier to return to later. When several pages belong together, saving them in one place and adding a recognizable title can help you find them again.

This recording shows three pages saved as one item in ZupZup, followed by applying a title and summary. The short edit starts with scanning the final page and continues in the saved item’s detail screen.

## Save several pages as one item

Scan the pages in sequence and save them to keep the scanned images together in one item. In the recording, the final page’s document area is highlighted before the saved item opens. The ‘1/3’ below its image shows that the first of three pages is displayed.

After saving, check the page count and image quality. Make sure no page is missing and that the text is not cropped or blurred. The presence of AI suggestions does not, by itself, confirm that every original page was captured clearly.

## Review the title and summary separately

The card labeled ‘정리 제안’ in the Korean interface contains title, summary, and tag suggestions. Its ‘AI 제안 · 확인 필요’ notice means that the AI suggestions need review. Read each suggestion before choosing what to use.

In the recording, tapping ‘적용’ (Apply) for the title replaces the date-based scan title at the top of the screen. Applying the summary separately puts it in ‘노트’ (Note). You do not have to accept both at once.

The summary in Note is separate from ‘인식한 원문’ (recognized text). It is an AI suggestion that condenses the content, so compare important details with the original images and recognized text. Tag suggestions are also visible, but the video does not show them being applied.

## What the analysis reads across pages

The app recognizes text from the saved scan images in order and combines that text for organizing suggestions. When the text is long, it uses the beginning of each image’s recognized text within a length limit. This does not guarantee a complete summary of every part of the document.

Image quality, language, and document layout can affect recognition. Check details such as numbers and dates against the original, and edit the title or note yourself if a suggestion is inaccurate.

## Interface language and AI requirements

The video uses the Korean interface. ZupZup’s menus support nine languages: Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish, German, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Text recognition and AI suggestions have their own language support and requirements, separate from the menu language.

AI organizing suggestions require iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later and an Apple Intelligence-compatible device. Apple Intelligence must be enabled, with its models ready. Check the automatic new-item analysis setting and the availability status shown in the app.

Free analysis is limited to 20 items per device per day, shared by new-item analysis and screenshot cleanup. Premium removes this daily limit, but the device and model requirements still apply. You can save documents and organize them manually without suggestions.

After saving a paper document, check three things: all the pages are there, the title helps you recognize the item, and the summary agrees with the original.
