This guide describes the workflow checked in ZupZup 1.15.

Sometimes you save an article in another language but do not have time to read it in full. If you want an overview in a familiar language before deciding what to read closely, try selecting a summary language in ZupZup.

## From saving a link to seeing the fetched body

The save form opened from Safari sharing shows the title and URL. The item detail then displays a webpage-body analysis status, followed by a saved-body link and Original Content. This recording shows the real state transition after saving, not a manual tap on a fetch command.

## Check the saved body first

Open Original Content before requesting a summary. This example, using the Korean interface, starts with paragraphs and images from an English article already present. A summary alone may not reveal that part of a page was never fetched, so check the saved content first.

## Choose the language

Open the action for summarizing in another language from the bottom menu. In this example, I selected Korean. This produces a shorter account of the saved body rather than translating the entire article.

The choices come from the app’s languages supported by the device’s model. Check which options are available on your device rather than assuming every language is supported.

## See progress and cancel if needed

A summary progress card appears in the detail view, with a cancel action. This example shows a single example. Processing time can vary with the text and device conditions.


![Summary progress and Cancel control](/media/zupzup-guides/web/summary-progress.png)

[Short excerpt of the summary processing state](/media/zupzup-guides/web/summary-progress.mp4)

## Keep the original and previous notes

When the summary finishes, a message confirms that it has been added to the note. In this example, the Korean summary appears beneath a date and language separator. The earlier English note remains, and Original Content is still available below it.

AI summaries can omit or misinterpret information. Compare important numbers, dates, names, and conclusions with the article. Long texts may include a processing-coverage notice; check whether any portion was omitted.

## Requirements and limits

A device and settings supporting Apple Intelligence and a ready model are required. Check language availability and the status shown in the app. Interface language support and summary-language support are separate.

This is one example of summarizing English text in Korean, not a test of every website or language pair. The original creators retain the rights to the text and images; check the source and reuse terms before publishing saved material.
