In 1.17, I revisited features I’d added over time and made them work together more consistently. The changes cover what happens after you save a screenshot or PDF, from choosing what to keep to opening it on another device.

### Review several screenshots in one go
After text recognition finishes, choose which lines to keep right on the image. Deselect anything you don’t need, like the time or battery percentage. Swipe through multiple screenshots, then confirm once. Confirmed text syncs through iCloud, so you can review it on your iPad and read it later on your iPhone.

### Recognize text in the PDF you choose
Open an item’s details to recognize text in an individual scanned PDF. If text is already available, Zupzup can use it to suggest how to organize the item. Automatic summaries of web links now follow the app’s display language. Organization suggestions and automatic summaries require Apple Intelligence.

### The daily free allowance now has a name: ZupChips
ZupChips are for optional features like organization suggestions. You don’t need them to save links or photos. Free users get 20 per device each day, replenished at midnight. You can see how many you have left in Settings. Lifetime Access removes the ZupChip limit.

You can also compare photos before and after reducing their file size, merge saved photo items, and see the progress and result when restoring purchases.
