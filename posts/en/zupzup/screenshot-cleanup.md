An explanation to read later, a product to look up again, a sentence worth keeping. Taking a screenshot is quick. Sorting out what comes next takes more work.

Open Photos and you may find rows of similar screens. Some are still useful; others have served their purpose. A long article captured across several screenshots may be scattered among them.

ZupZup’s Screenshot Cleanup lets you choose what to do with each photo, then review those choices together. You do not have to tackle your entire photo library at once.

## About the example screens

The example screens use Korean. This article uses the corresponding English menu labels. ZupZup’s interface supports Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish, German, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Interface languages are separate from the language support for text recognition and AI suggestions.

## Start with a small batch

In ZupZup, open Tools → Screenshot Cleanup. After granting photo access, you can browse newest or oldest first, or narrow the batch to a date range.

![ZupZup Screenshot Cleanup start screen with newest-first, oldest-first, and date-range options.](/media/zupzup-guides/cleanup/01-cleanup-start.png)

*Choose an order or date range before starting.*

Try a small range, such as the last few days. Each photo has three choices:

- Skip: leave the decision for another time.

- Delete: select the photo for deletion without keeping a copy in ZupZup.

- Keep: select the photo to keep a copy in ZupZup.

Tapping Delete here does not immediately delete the photo. Keep does not finish saving it, either. Make your selections first, check them in Final Review, and then run the cleanup.

[Silent clip of choosing Skip, Delete, or Keep while reviewing screenshots, with account identifiers hidden.](/media/zupzup-guides/cleanup/02-classify-review-account-redacted.mp4)

*Review screenshots one at a time and choose what to do with them. Actions run after Final Review.*

## Keep related screenshots in one item

If a long explanation spans several screenshots, keeping them together may be more useful than saving each as a separate item.

In Final Review, you can combine several photos marked Keep into one item. They are arranged by capture time, and the first photo becomes the main image.

For example, a three-screen set of instructions can stay together so you can read it in sequence later. There is no need to group unrelated photos.

## When you are not sure what to call it

With Auto-Organize Text selected, ZupZup can read the text in saved images and suggest a title, summary, and tags. A new item’s default title may be filled in automatically when the conditions are met. In the Suggested Cleanup card, you can review and apply each part, and choose only the tags you need.

Small or blurry text can affect recognition. Check that the title and summary match the original before applying them.

Automatic organizing suggestions require iOS 26 or later, or iPadOS 26 or later, on a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with it enabled and the model ready. You can still keep photos when analysis is unavailable or you have used the free allowance.

The free allowance is 20 items per device per day, shared between automatic analysis of new items and Screenshot Cleanup. Several photos grouped into one item count as one item. Premium removes the daily limit on automatic organizing suggestions.

This allowance is separate from the number of photos you can save or sort.

## Check original deletion separately

Keeping a copy in ZupZup and deleting the original from Photos are separate choices. Also Delete Originals After Saving is a separate option, and it is off by default.

For a first try, leave it off and keep just a few photos. Open the saved photos in ZupZup to check that they are organized as you expected.

If you choose to delete originals, ZupZup reads the saved copy again to verify it before requesting deletion. That check does not mean the iCloud upload has finished. Deletion also requires full photo-library access, confirmation in ZupZup, and the system’s photo-deletion confirmation.

A photo marked only Delete will not leave a copy in ZupZup. If you use iCloud Photos, deletion may also affect your other devices and other participants in a Shared Photo Library. Check the final list before proceeding.

*Check which originals will be deleted after saving and which will be deleted without saving.*

## You can finish another day

Choose Continue Later when you want to pause. Return to Continue Cleanup to review the remaining photos. The cleanup session is stored only on this device and does not sync to other devices.

Start with a few photos. Keeping the useful ones together and skipping those you are not ready to decide on can make them easier to find later.
