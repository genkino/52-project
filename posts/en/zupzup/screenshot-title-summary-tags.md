Saving a screenshot takes a moment. But when similar screens pile up in Photos, you have to open each one again to remember what it contains. Titles and tags make things easier to find, though adding them by hand takes time too.

For this example, I tried ZupZup’s Suggested Cleanup with a screenshot containing text. It uses the text read from the image to suggest a title, summary, and tags. You can review and apply each part separately.

## About the example screens

The screenshots and video show the Korean interface. This article uses the corresponding English menu labels. ZupZup’s interface supports nine language options: Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish, German, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

Text recognition and AI suggestions have their own supported languages and requirements, separate from the interface language. The device requirements for AI suggestions are explained below.

## A screenshot saved through the share menu

In this example, the screenshot was saved to ZupZup through the share menu, with Analyze New Items Automatically enabled. The results appear in the Suggested Cleanup card on the saved image’s detail screen.

The screen initially showed ‘이미지’ (Image) at the top. The suggestions included the title ‘낙관적 허무주의’ (optimistic nihilism), a paragraph-length summary, and three tags: ‘마음’, ‘낙관적’, and ‘허무’. The last two were marked as new tags.

The card also indicates that these are AI suggestions that need review. It gives you a starting point for choosing a title and deciding how to summarize the content.

## Read the title and summary before applying them

In the video, applying the title changed ‘이미지’ at the top of the screen to ‘낙관적 허무주의’. The summary was applied separately and then appeared under Note. You do not have to accept the title and summary together.

The summary is an AI-generated suggestion, not a verbatim copy of the original. It is worth checking it against Recognized Text and the original image. If a word was misread or the summary makes a stronger claim than the source, you can leave it unapplied.

Each part also has a Reject button. If a suggestion does not fit, you can skip it and write a title or note that will help you find the item later.

## Choose the tags you need

All three tags start out selected. In the video, ‘낙관적’ and ‘허무’ were deselected, leaving only ‘마음’. This is useful when an existing tag is enough and you do not want to create two more.

Changing the checkmarks only chooses which tags to apply. You still need to tap Apply in the tags section to add the selected tags to the item. The video shows the selection with only ‘마음’ remaining; it does not show that final application.

[Silent edited clip showing title and summary application followed by deselecting two of three suggested tags.](/media/zupzup-guides/image/zupzup-recommend-and-apply-10s.mp4)

*Apply the title and summary, then select only ‘마음’ from the suggested tags. The final tag application is not shown.*

You do not have to keep every suggested word. Think about the term you would use to find this item later, and keep the tags you need.

## Before using automatic analysis

Suggested Cleanup requires iOS 26 or later, or iPadOS 26 or later, on a device that supports Apple Intelligence. Apple Intelligence must be enabled and the on-device model ready. Check Analyze New Items Automatically and the availability information shown in the app first.

The free allowance is 20 items per device per day. Automatic analysis of new items and Screenshot Cleanup share this allowance, which resets at midnight according to the device’s time. An item counts when a suggestion is generated and saved, whether or not you apply it later. Premium removes this daily limit, but the device and model requirements still apply.

This example shows the result for one screenshot containing text. Other images will not necessarily produce the same title or the same number of tags. You can still save images and organize them by hand without suggestions.

## Leave yourself a clue for next time

You do not have to organize everything perfectly at once. Apply a useful title first, read the summary before deciding, and choose only the tags you need.

The next time you save a screenshot of something you want to read again, open Suggested Cleanup. Replacing ‘Image’ with a title that tells you what is inside is a useful place to start.
