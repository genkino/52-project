Some blog posts are worth revisiting; others are useful because you want to remember why you saved them, perhaps for a presentation or a class. You do not have to organize both kinds in the same way.

## Keep posts you want to reread in Gatherly

You can save a post in Gatherly and open it again later. It is a simple way to collect things you want to reread without adding notes or tags. For more on adding a blog and reading its posts in a feed, see [Read new blog posts in one place with Gatherly](https://blog.52proj.com/en/gatherly/gatherly-first-read/).

![A public blog post open in Gatherly](/images/gatherly-zupzup-save/gatherly-article.png)

* This screen shows a public post open inside Gatherly. It does not show the save action itself.

## Use ZupZup when you want to remember why you saved something

If you want a reminder of why you saved a post, you can send it to ZupZup. Open the post in Gatherly, bring up the iOS share sheet, and choose ZupZup. The original URL is passed to ZupZup's save form.

![The iOS share sheet with ZupZup selected](/images/gatherly-zupzup-save/ios-share-sheet.png)

* The iOS share sheet shown when sharing a Gatherly post.

Check that the URL is right, then add a note about why you want to revisit it. For example, “For next week’s presentation” gives you a useful clue. If you have already created tags in ZupZup, you can choose one to group the item.

![ZupZup's save form showing the URL passed from Gatherly](/images/gatherly-zupzup-save/zupzup-save-form.png)

* The URL has been passed into the save form. The note is still blank, and tags can be selected after you create them in ZupZup.

## Find it with words from your note

To look for an item by a phrase in its note, switch the search scope to “Notes” and enter the phrase. The next screen shows one result for “For next week’s presentation.” It is a separate item called “Blog Collection,” not the same link shown in the share flow.

![A ZupZup search result for words in a note](/images/gatherly-zupzup-save/note-search-example.png)

* The result is a note on a separately saved “Blog Collection” item.

If you only want to read a post again, keep it in Gatherly. If you want to find something by its purpose or topic, add a note or tag in ZupZup. You do not need to move everything.

Saving a URL does not permanently preserve the entire original webpage. For anything important, check both the saved item and the original page. Learn more on the [Gatherly App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6756641122) and the [ZupZup App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758145905).
